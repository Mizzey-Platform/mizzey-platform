## Risks

**US-16-02, Every product field is editable.** Woo carries almost all of AC 1 natively, but compare at price, video in the product gallery and the AC 4 badges are not native fields and no section 7 row names any of them. The classification is extend rather than native because those additions exist, and extend rather than build because the product editor Woo ships is a real base.

**US-16-03, Product content in both languages.** P-006 proves the data model carries translated products, variations and attributes as one translation group, so claimed native is wrong: WordPress is single language and this story exists only because a licensed plugin supplies the capability. AC 3, the missing content report, is not delivered by the plugin, and it must resolve translation groups on icl_translations rather than count posts, because P-019 showed a product level query reads one product as two.

**US-16-05, Product cost captured from day one.** Both halves are proved native. P-016 found a native Cost of Goods Sold field on products that survives save and reload, and P-017 found the cost frozen onto the order line during calculate_totals, an order for 2 units at cost 100 still reading 200 after the product cost was changed to 999. The whole risk is that the feature is off by default, an order placed while it is off carries no cost at all, and enabling it later does not backfill. That is now a go live gate check rather than a build task.
