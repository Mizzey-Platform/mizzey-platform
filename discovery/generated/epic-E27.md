## Risks

**US-27-04, Data integrity.** Four probes bear on this story and three of them landed. P-002: an order for 3 units at 100 still read 300 after the product price was raised to 250, so BR-005 is native. P-014: the refund ceiling held, so BR-006 is native. P-004: a repeated callback against the same order was a genuine no-operation, so half of AC 2 is native. Extend on that basis. The fourth is the problem. P-009 is PARTIAL and it is the one that matters for AC 3: it established that the stock write is atomic per statement but does not refuse to go negative, and it could not test true simultaneity because one PHP process cannot place two checkouts at the same instant. So AC 3, concurrent purchases of the last unit resulting in one sale, is the single criterion in this story that no probe has settled, and it is cited as evidence here only so the gap is visible rather than absent.
