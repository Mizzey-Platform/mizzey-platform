## Risks

**US-07-06, Stock validation at placement.** Claimed native, and native is not earned. P-009 established the mechanism: wc_update_product_stock delegates to the data store, the data store issues arithmetic SQL so the write is atomic per statement, but two sequential decreases of 1 from a stock of 1 left the quantity at minus 1. The write does not refuse to go negative. The guard against overselling is a validation step before payment, separate from the write, so a check-then-write gap exists in principle. P-009 could NOT settle the concurrent case, because one PHP process cannot create simultaneity, and it is booked as a parallel load test at issue #233 in sprint 8, the same sprint as this story. Section 6 calls this Native and section 7 has no row for it, so if the load test fails there is no gap row to hang the remedial work on. That absence is the finding.

**US-07-07, Duplicate order and duplicate charge protection.** Claimed native, and P-004 shows native covers one of the two cases. Woo carries a real guard on the order side: payment_complete called twice with the same reference left status processing, date_paid unchanged and stock reduced once from 10 to 9, because _order_stock_reduced is set on the first pass and respected on the second. That is AC 2. What P-004 explicitly did not establish is AC 1: Woo does nothing about a customer submitting checkout twice and creating two separate orders, and nothing about a gateway retrying against an order that was meanwhile cancelled. Both are integrator work, so section 7 row 12 is right that this belongs to Mizzey, and the probe narrows what is left rather than clearing it.

## Open questions

- **US-07-06.** AC 4, no order created in a state that reserves more stock than exists, is exactly what P-009 could not settle. Acceptance of this story should wait on issue #233 rather than being signed off in sprint 8 on the validation step alone.
- **US-07-06.** Flagged under at 3 points. AC 3 requires the customer to be told exactly which line is affected and what quantity is available, which is per line reporting plus bilingual copy at the checkout failure moment. If #233 shows a real race, a locking or reservation strategy lands on top of that and 3 points is well short.
- **US-07-06.** No section 7 row covers stock concurrency. If remedial work is needed it has no gap row, and section 6 asserting Native is the only place the claim lives.
- **US-07-07.** Flagged under at 3 points. AC 1 double submission, AC 3 holding across a page refresh and the back button during payment, and AC 4 recording the duplicate attempt are three separate mechanisms, and only AC 2 is native. AC 3 in particular means an idempotency key surviving a browser navigation, not a disabled button.
- **US-07-07.** AC 4 requires the duplicate attempt to be recorded for diagnosis. That is the audit seam at section 7 row 14, which belongs to E19 and E21. Whether it exists by sprint 8 is a sequencing question, and this story is in Stage 3 while the audit work is later.
- **US-07-07.** The gateway retry against a cancelled order, named by P-004 and by no acceptance criterion, has no home. It is a real production failure mode and PAY-05 is broad enough to cover it, but no AC says so.
