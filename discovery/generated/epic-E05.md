## Risks

**US-05-09, Cart persists for signed in customers.** P-001 observed the mechanism directly: the test user carried a saved persistent cart holding product 40, which is WooCommerce user meta and is what makes AC 1 and AC 2 native. Cross-device persistence for a signed in customer is therefore not built. What is built is AC 3: Woo revalidates prices when the cart reloads but does not tell the customer that a price moved, so the "shown as changed rather than applied silently" half is Mizzey work. Extend rather than native for that reason. No section 7 row covers it, which is correct, because row 1 is about the guest merge and this story is about the signed in cart.

**US-05-10, Guest cart survives registration.** P-001 is direct evidence: guest session cart held product 39, the account had a saved cart holding product 40, and after login exactly one cart survived, the guest one. WooCommerce replaces rather than merges, so every one of CART-13 to CART-18 is Mizzey work and section 7 row 1 is right to flag it. The probe also confirmed woocommerce_cart_loaded_from_session is a real action in 11.1.0, so the hook the Technical Design builds on exists. The registration path is the simplest of the three because the new account has no prior cart to reconcile, which is why 3 points is defensible where US-05-11 is 8.

**US-05-11, Guest cart merges on sign in.** The same P-001 evidence, and this is the story it bites hardest. Woo replaces rather than merges and, in the probe's words, whichever side is dropped is dropped silently, which is what makes this the delicate one. AC 3, one line for the same product and variant, and AC 4, capping at available stock and telling the customer, are both entirely Mizzey. AC 4 also depends on the stock question in US-07-06: capping needs a trustworthy available figure, and P-009 showed the stock write does not refuse to go negative.

**US-05-12, Guest cart survives Google sign in.** Rows 1 and 16 together: the merge is Mizzey work and Google sign in is Plugin or Build, still undecided at PL-02. Not marked proved: P-001 tested the ordinary login path by becoming the user and reloading the cart from session, not an OAuth callback. The merge code is shared with US-05-11, so the classification follows, but the thing that could break here is whether the chosen Google sign in plugin fires woocommerce_cart_loaded_from_session at all on its callback. If it signs the user in outside that hook the merge never runs and the guest cart is lost silently, which is the exact failure P-001 describes. 2 points holds only if the plugin behaves; the risk is in the undecided plugin, not in the story.

## Open questions

- **US-05-09.** The change notice in AC 3 is customer facing and bilingual, and no copy exists for it. It also needs a decision: is a price rise shown and the item kept, or shown and the item removed for re-confirmation?
- **US-05-09.** AC 1 says the same cart is present on another device. Woo restores the persistent cart into the session on login, but if the second device already has a guest session cart the merge rules of US-05-11 apply. The two stories overlap at that point and neither says which one owns the behaviour.
- **US-05-10.** AC 3 requires the behaviour to hold whether registration starts from the cart, the header or the checkout. Those are three entry points with different session states, and the acceptance criteria do not say whether a part-completed checkout counts as a fourth.
- **US-05-11.** AC 4 requires the customer to be told what changed when a quantity is capped. Bilingual copy, and it appears mid sign in, which is a moment with no existing message surface.
- **US-05-11.** The merge rule itself is undecided: when the same variant is in both carts at quantity 2 and 3, is the result 3 (the greater) or 5 (the sum)? AC 3 says one line and AC 2 says nothing is lost, and those two readings conflict. This needs settling before sprint 6, not during it.
- **US-05-11.** P-015 established that a guest wishlist faces the identical replace-versus-merge decision, and recommended designing the two together. That is a sequencing note for US-11-04, which is not in this batch.
- **US-05-12.** Which plugin satisfies PL-02 is undecided, and this story's estimate depends on it. Worth one probe once a candidate is chosen: does its sign in path fire woocommerce_cart_loaded_from_session? That is cheap and it de-risks US-05-12, US-06-03 and US-06-04 at once.
- **US-05-12.** AC 2 requires an existing account matched by email to be signed into rather than duplicated. That is the US-06-04 invariant reached through a different door, and the two stories should not both build it.
