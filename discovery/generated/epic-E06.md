## Risks

**US-06-03, Sign in and register with Google.** Section 7 row 16 classes Google sign in as Plugin or Build and sizes it Small, and PL-02 carries the need with decision still null. AC 1, AC 2 and AC 5 are what an OAuth plugin delivers. Classified plugin on that basis. AC 3, no second account for an existing email, is not a plugin behaviour and belongs to US-06-04, and AC 4 is US-05-12. This story is thin once those two are removed, which is why 8 points is left at ok rather than flagged over: the number only makes sense if it is carrying part of the identity resolution, and if it is not, three stories are pointing the same work.

**US-06-04, One account per email.** The plugin supplies OAuth; nothing supplies identity resolution. AC 4 is the load bearing one and it is an invariant over every route: manual registration, guest checkout linking, and Google sign in. WooCommerce enforces unique user emails at the WordPress level, so a literal duplicate is refused, but that is not what AC 4 asks. It asks that the routes resolve to one account rather than failing, and AC 3 requires the resolution to be explained to the customer instead of erroring. That is build, on top of the plugin.

**US-06-07, One step account creation after ordering.** Section 7 row 20 names AUTH-11 exactly: Woo offers account creation at checkout, not after it from the confirmation with no re entry, and it is classed Build and sized Small. AC 4 is the part that is not small in risk terms even if it is in effort: a guest order must not be claimable by an account that does not own the ordering email. That is the same trust boundary as US-06-04 AC 3, approached from the order side.

## Open questions

- **US-06-03.** AC 3 here and AC 2 of US-06-04 are the same requirement written twice. Which story owns identity resolution has to be settled before sprint 7 or it is built twice or not at all.
- **US-06-03.** Row 16 leaves Plugin or Build genuinely open and PL-02 has no candidate. Until one is named, neither this estimate nor US-05-12's can be trusted.
- **US-06-03.** AC 5 excludes social login beyond Google as P2. Nothing here contradicts that, recorded only so the exclusion is visible in the dataset.
- **US-06-04.** AC 4 cannot be verified story by story. It is a property of the set of routes, and one of those routes, guest checkout linking, is US-06-07 in the same sprint. Acceptance for this story should wait on that one.
- **US-06-04.** AC 3 requires an explanation to the customer. Merging an anonymous Google identity into an existing password account is a security sensitive moment, and whether it requires proof of ownership of the existing account, such as email verification, is not stated. Resolving silently would let anyone holding a Google account for an address take over the store account for it.
- **US-06-04.** The account takeover question above is worth deciding with Mustafa rather than at build time. It is the kind of thing that is cheap to specify now and expensive to retrofit.
- **US-06-07.** AC 4 needs a stated mechanism. Claiming on the confirmation page within the session is one thing; a link in the confirmation email is another; allowing it later from an account is a third and is the one that leaks. Nothing says which is intended.
- **US-06-07.** AC 5 says declining leaves the order reachable through the confirmation email. That implies a guest order lookup route, which no story in this batch owns and which is itself an access control surface.
- **US-06-07.** BR-008 is in the trace and is not read here. Whether it constrains the linking window, for example claim only within N days, should be checked before sprint 7.
