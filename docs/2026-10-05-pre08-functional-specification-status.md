## 4. Classification

**PRE-08 is an acceptance gate. It is not a development blocker.**

| Question | Answer | The words that decide it |
|---|---|---|
| Does its approval have to come before development starts? | **No** | "approved before Stage 2 is accepted". The register uses different words where it means a start condition: PRE-07 says "before development begins", PRE-03b says "approved before storefront implementation begins", PRE-09 says "before dependent work begins". PRE-08 says none of these |
| Does it gate a stage? | **Yes, Stage 2 acceptance** | "approved before Stage 2 is accepted" |
| Does it gate money? | **Yes, every remaining payment** | "no Stage 2 or later payment falls due until it has been passed" |
| Does it gate production? | **Yes** | DOD-09, "Client approval of acceptance criteria before a feature moves to production" |
| Does the Project Plan make it a start condition for Stage 2? | **No** | Project Plan 3.2, "Needed from you before it starts", lists the interface design approval, the hosting instruction, the category structure and the sample export. It does not list the specification |

This matches the engineering record. `platform/DECISIONS.md` D-10 places "client approval (DOD-09) throughout" under
"Blocks final acceptance", and `PROGRESS.md` reports every merged feature as technically verified and not
contractually accepted.

One caution on "no development blocker". What We Are Building, MS-DOC-2026-007 section 9, tells the client: "Before
development begins we deliver and you approve: the technical design, the technology stack confirmation, the
interface designs, and the project breakdown." It does not name the specification, and it is not part of the
Agreement (section 23). It is still what the client was told to expect.

## 6. A point that needs a decision, not an assumption

Two clauses pull in different directions, and the dates are not on file.

* Statement of Work 4.2 requires approval "in writing, on the Stage 1 Approval Record".
* Agreement 6.4 treats "anything submitted for review" as approved after 10 Working Days of silence, and CR-13 says "Deliverable deemed approved".

A Working Day is "Sunday to Thursday, excluding official public holidays in Egypt". **If** the specification counts
as submitted on 22 September 2026, the tenth Working Day falls on about 6 or 7 October 2026, depending on how the
6 October holiday is observed. That is this week. I could not confirm the submission date, the client's signature
date, or whether the period runs before the Agreement was signed, so this is a calculation on an assumed date and
nothing more.

It would be unwise to rely on silence for this document. The record requires a signature, the environments are not
set up, and the specification has corrections outstanding that the developer already knows about. The Agreement
states it has not been reviewed by a lawyer; this note is not legal advice either.
