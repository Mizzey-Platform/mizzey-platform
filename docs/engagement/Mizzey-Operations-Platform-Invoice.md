## 1. How to use this template

This is the invoice for the Mizzey Operations Platform engagement. **One invoice per payment**, seven in total: six stage payments and one for the interface design.

The fields on this page are fillable. Open the PDF in any reader, click a field and type. Nothing else on the page can be edited by accident.

## 9. What has to be true before each invoice is sent

An invoice is only correct if the thing it bills for has actually happened. This is the check.

| # | Send only when |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Agreement and the Statement of Work are signed by both parties |
| 2 | The client has approved the interface design **in writing** |
| 3 | Stage 2 is accepted, or 5 working days have passed since it was submitted with no written response |
| 4 | Stage 3 is accepted, or the 5 working days have passed |
| 5 | Stage 4 is accepted, or the 5 working days have passed |
| 6 | Stage 5 is accepted, or the 5 working days have passed |
| 7 | Acceptance is signed under MS-UAT-2026-013 and the store is live |

**Acceptance by silence is real and it counts.** MS-SOW-2026-009 section 4.1 says that if no written response arrives within 5 working days of a stage being submitted, the stage is treated as accepted and its payment falls due. Keep the email that submitted it: the date on that email is what starts the 5 days.
