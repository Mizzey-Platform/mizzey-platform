## Slide 02. What Mizzey will be

A premium store, not a marketplace and not a discount site.

| # | Item | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | A store, not a marketplace | You sell your own products. There are no external sellers, no commissions and no seller accounts to manage. |
| 02 | Premium, not discount | The presentation, the imagery and even the way offers appear are built to signal authenticity and quality rather than cheapness. |
| 03 | Run by you, not by a developer | Products, prices, offers, home page and orders are all yours to change. You should not need us for daily business. |

> **English primary · Arabic fully supported from launch**
>
> English is the primary and default storefront language, and the interface is designed English-first. Arabic is fully supported at launch: the same pages and the same functions, with complete right to left layouts and Arabic typography. Arabic is not a later phase and not a reduced version.

## Slide 04. Getting an account without losing the sale

Three ways in, and the cart survives all of them.

| # | Item | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Guest checkout | Buy with no account at all. The order can be linked to an account later. |
| 02 | Sign in with Google | One tap. No password to invent, no form to fill. |
| 03 | Register normally | Email and password, and the welcome discount applies. |

> **The cart is never lost**
>
> A guest fills a cart, then registers: nothing is lost. Signs in halfway through: the old and new carts merge with no duplicates. Comes back tomorrow on a different device: the cart is still there. This sounds small. It is one of the most common places online stores quietly lose sales.

## Slide 05. Your daily operations screen

One page that tells you what needs doing today.

### What it shows

- Orders needing action, worked top to bottom
- Stock needing attention: low, out, missing an image, unpublished
- Returns waiting for your decision
- Today’s orders, revenue and units

### What you can do from it

- Add a product in under three minutes
- Change price or stock across many products at once
- Send many orders to the courier and print all labels together
- Find any order by customer phone number
- Save a filtered view and return to it in one click

> **Note**
>
> The admin panel is in English. Your training and admin manual are in Arabic.

## Slide 07. Bringing your Amazon catalogue across

A tool you can run yourself, as often as you need.

| Stage | Step | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Upload | Your Amazon export file |
| 2 | Map | Tell it which column means what, once |
| 3 | Check | See exactly what will happen before anything changes |
| 4 | Import | Products created, with Arabic text and variations handled |

> **One thing to start early**
>
> The basic Amazon export contains only titles, prices, codes and one image. Full descriptions and product variations come from a different report that Amazon does not switch on by default. You need to ask Amazon Seller Support to enable it.

## Slide 10. Loyalty, VIP tiers and referrals

Future opportunities, not in the current contract. Specified now so they can be quoted quickly when you want them.

| # | Item | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| P3 | Loyalty points | Customers earn points on delivered orders and spend them as a discount, with balance and history in their account. Advanced phase in your requirements document. |
| P3 | VIP tiers | Tiers based on spending, each with its own benefits, and visible progress to the next tier. Advanced phase in your requirements document. |
| P2 | Referral programme | Each customer gets a link, and both sides are rewarded once the new customer’s first order completes. Growth phase in your requirements document. |

> **Deliberately post-launch, and not out of reach**
>
> These are intentionally separate post-launch growth features. Loyalty thresholds and earning rules are better set using real customer and order behaviour than assumptions made before launch. All three are specified in full in the Feature Register, and the data model carries their fields from day one, so any of them can be brought forward by agreement as separate work and quoted against a written specification rather than a fresh analysis.
