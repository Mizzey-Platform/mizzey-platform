## 2. What this Agreement covers

The Client wishes to have an independent e-commerce platform built for the Mizzey business. The Developer has proposed three packages, and the Client has selected the **Mizzey Operations Platform**.

This Agreement sets out the terms on which that platform is built, paid for, accepted, owned and supported.

### 2.1 The documents that make up this Agreement

This Agreement consists of the terms below together with the annexes listed in section 22. All of them apply together.

If they conflict, they are read in this order of priority:

1. This Agreement
2. Annex A, the Feature Register, for anything about **scope**
3. Annex B, the Statement of Work, for anything about **money or dates**
4. The remaining annexes

Nothing said in conversation, in a meeting, in a message or in an earlier draft forms part of this Agreement unless it appears in one of these documents or in an agreed Change Request.

## 4. Scope of the services

### 4.1 What the Developer will do

The Developer will design, build, test, deploy and hand over the Platform, and will produce the written deliverables listed in Annex A as DLV rows.

The Developer will also produce the Interface Design, to the requirements in the Design Brief and Request for Proposal, MS-RFP-2026-003. The Client has confirmed that the Developer provides it. **Brand identity is not part of the Interface Design and is not included**, as Annex B section 9.1 sets out.

### 4.2 Scope is defined by Annex A, and only by Annex A

**Only rows marked P1, P1-L or DLV in Annex A create a delivery obligation.** Rows marked P2, P3 or OUT are recorded for completeness and create no obligation, no price, no date and no warranty.

This is the rule that prevents a disagreement about scope from becoming a disagreement about memory. If something is expected, it should be found in Annex A before signature, and if it is not there it can be added there before signature.

### 4.3 Second release items

Some items are contracted but delivered shortly after Production Go-Live. These are marked **S2** in Annex A. They are included in the Fee and are not a separate purchase. Launch is not the end of this Agreement.

### 4.4 Standard of work

The Developer will perform the services with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with current professional engineering practice.

## 9. Intellectual property and ownership

This section decides who owns what. It matters more than any other section in this Agreement, so it is written precisely.

### 9.1 Mizzey-Specific Work belongs to the Client

On payment of the Fee in full, all Mizzey-Specific Work belongs to the Client. This includes the source code, configuration, theme and templates, database schema and migrations, documentation and project assets created specifically for the Client.

The Mizzey-Specific Work is delivered throughout the project to **a repository owned by the Client**, with its development history, not handed over as a single archive at the end.

### 9.2 Open source and third-party components

WordPress, WooCommerce, and any third-party plugins, libraries or components used in the Platform remain subject to their own licences. The Client does not acquire ownership of them, and neither does the Developer. This is normal and applies to every project built on open source.

### 9.3 Corex

Corex is the reusable framework and application layer of the Developer, built over several projects and used here to avoid rebuilding foundations from nothing. **Corex remains the property of the Developer.**

On payment of the Fee in full, the Developer grants the Client a licence to use Corex as incorporated in the Platform. That licence is:

* **perpetual**, it does not expire
* **irrevocable**, it cannot be withdrawn, including if this Agreement ends or the Parties fall out
* **royalty-free**, there is nothing further to pay for it, ever
* **non-exclusive**, the Developer continues to use Corex on other projects
* **worldwide**, and not limited to Egypt
* extending to the right to **use, host, run, modify and have modified** Corex as part of the Platform
* extending to **any other developer or agency the Client engages**, who may operate, maintain and extend the Platform, including the Corex layer within it, on behalf of the Client

The one thing the licence does not permit is extracting Corex from the Platform and selling, licensing or distributing it as a standalone product to a third party.

The practical effect, stated plainly: **the Client is not locked in.** The Client can take the Platform to any other qualified development team at any time, and that team can run and extend all of it. What the Client cannot do is resell the framework itself as a product, which is not something the Client has any reason to do.

### 9.4 What the Client owns from the start

The Mizzey brand, name, logo, content, product data, images, customer data and business information belong to the Client at all times, and nothing in this Agreement changes that.

### 9.5 Before full payment

Until the Fee is paid in full, the licences and transfers in this section have not taken effect. Work delivered to the repository of the Client before then is delivered for review and testing.

### 9.6 Portfolio

The Developer may describe this project and show images of the delivered interface in a professional portfolio, with the prior written approval of the Client, which will not be unreasonably refused. No commercial figures, customer data or confidential information may be shown.

## 10. Warranty

### 10.1 What is warranted

The Developer warrants the Platform for **60 calendar days from Production Go-Live**.

During the Warranty Period the Developer will correct, at no charge, reproducible defects where the delivered implementation does not conform to the scope in Annex A and the acceptance criteria in Annex C.

### 10.2 What the warranty is not

**The warranty is not a service level agreement.** It carries no guaranteed response time, no uptime commitment and no availability guarantee.

It does not cover:

* faults in third-party services, including hosting, the payment provider, the courier and any third-party plugin
* changes made to the Platform by the Client or anyone else after handover
* new requirements, new features and changes of mind, which are Change Requests
* content, data or configuration errors introduced by the Client
* problems caused by the Client not following the documented operating procedures

### 10.3 After the Warranty Period

Support after the Warranty Period is optional, on the terms in Annex E. There is no mandatory retainer, no minimum term, and no requirement to buy support from the Developer.

## 17. Termination

### 17.1 By either Party for breach

Either Party may end this Agreement in writing if the other is in material breach and has not corrected it within **15 Working Days** of written notice describing the breach.

### 17.2 By the Client for convenience

The Client may end this Agreement at any time on **10 Working Days** written notice.

### 17.3 What happens on termination

* The Client pays for every Stage accepted, and a fair proportion of the Stage in progress, based on the work actually performed.
* The Developer delivers the Mizzey-Specific Work completed to that point to the repository of the Client, together with the credentials, documentation and access needed to continue.
* On payment of the amounts due, the ownership and licence terms in section 9 apply to everything delivered, **including the Corex licence for what has been delivered.** The Client is never left holding code it cannot lawfully run.
* Confidentiality, intellectual property, limitation of liability and governing law survive termination.

## 18. Force majeure

The affected Party will notify the other promptly, and the timeline extends by the period affected. If the event continues for more than **60 days**, either Party may end this Agreement under section 17.3.
