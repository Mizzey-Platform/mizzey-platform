## 1. Purpose of this meeting

You have selected the **Mizzey Operations Platform** at **310,000 EGP**, being 290,000 for the platform and 20,000 for the interface design.

This meeting exists to turn that selection into an agreement, and to settle the decisions that the first two weeks of work depend on. It is not a sales meeting, and nothing in it is intended to reopen the choice between the three packages.

By the end of it there should be a signed Statement of Work, a start date, and a named person on each side.
