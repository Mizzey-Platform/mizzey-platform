# Analysis and review record: product reviews

Written and built by Claude Code (lead). Codex (gpt-6.1-sol, read-only) reviewed as a second opinion. Every finding
was checked against WordPress's and WooCommerce 11.1.0's source before it was applied, and each fix has a t61 check that
fails when the fix is removed (`evidence/mutation.txt`), unless the record says why it cannot have one.

| Round | Verdict | Findings, and what was done |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | FIX FIRST | One High, seven Medium, one Low. WooCommerce's REST reviews endpoint skipped the rule and let staff without moderation rights publish: guarded, and only staff who moderate may set a status. A second review could arrive through the REST comments endpoint as a plain comment, and WPML narrowed the duplicate check to one language: the check counts every review type in both languages, and REST reviews are stored as reviews. Closing comments hid the whole Reviews tab: the tab and approved reviews stay, and the form shows only the reason to someone who may not review. A review without a rating could be stored: every route needs a rating of 1 to 5, and REST keeps it. A line refunded in full by amount alone still counted: not any more. A subject staff set, translated by WooCommerce Multilingual, was bypassed: their path is kept, and the defaults follow the order's language. Two deliveries at once could send two invitations: one, under a lock per order. Two products with one name lost a link: one link per product |
| 2 | FIX FIRST | One High, five Medium. WooCommerce's REST review updates and batches could publish a held review: only staff who moderate may change a review or set its status there. A non-moderator's review there was stored against nobody, under any name: bound to their account. The reason did not show to signed-in customers: it shows in each state. A delivered line that cost nothing did not count: it does. The invitation mark could be read from a cached order: read from storage under the lock. Two submissions at once could both pass the one-review check: the check and the insert are held under a lock per customer and product |
| 3 | FIX FIRST | Two High, one Medium, all fixed. WooCommerce's older nested routes (`/wc/v1` and `/wc/v2` `/products/{id}/reviews`) inserted a review with none of the checks: only staff who moderate may write through them. WordPress's `/wp/v2/comments/{id}` let someone who may edit the product approve a review: refused for a comment on a product, or one moved onto a product. The review lock took the first id of the translation group, which differs by language: it takes the lowest |
| 4 | FIX FIRST | Two High, both fixed. A review's id in the body of `/wp/v2/comments/{other}` overrode the address, and the admin's reply with approval approved a held review for anyone who may edit the product: editing a product's review now needs the right to moderate (`edit_comment`, which every handler that changes a comment checks), and the REST guard reads the id the controller uses |
| 5 | READY | No High or Medium findings |
