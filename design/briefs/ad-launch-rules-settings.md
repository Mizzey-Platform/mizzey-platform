## Purpose

Lets staff set the launch rules: the welcome discount's value, cap, expiry and eligibility, the free-shipping condition for two or more items, and whether the welcome discount, a coupon and free shipping can combine.

Flows it serves: Staff: promotions, rules and merchandising (`S05`).
