## Purpose

Lets staff create automatic promotions or coupon codes with their conditions, rewards, limits and exclusions and the combination setting, schedule their start and end, and pause or deactivate one without deleting its usage history.

Flows it serves: Staff: promotions, rules and merchandising (`S05`).
