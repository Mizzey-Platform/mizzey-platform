# Second-release wireframes

Batch-10, group 10-A, is drawn and reviewed: `batch-10/` holds the five customer-facing second-release surfaces (filter by
rating, brand landing, reorder, notification preferences, account deletion), English and Arabic, accepted by the
owner on 27 October 2026. Group 10-B, the three administration additions (customer and conversion dashboard
figures, customer metrics and the personal data export, the customer email template editor), English only, was
accepted on 28 October 2026. Batch-10 is closed.

The contracted second-release surfaces are not part of the first wireframe pass. They get their own low-fidelity
pass here, after the first-release pass and once the structural component and interaction system is stable, and
before PRE-03b is approved (DQ-04). They are designed here and built in the second release.

The format is in [../README.md](../README.md), and the workflow in [../workflow.md](../workflow.md).
