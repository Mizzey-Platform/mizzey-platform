ac-reorder Reorder from order history

Lets a returning customer repeat a previous order from their order history in one action: a cart is built from its items at current availability and current prices, and items no longer available are reported rather than silently left out.

ac-reorder · default

The action where it applies and absent where it does not: on the detail of a delivered order (O-3), where WooCommerce places “Order again”; not on the order card (A4), not on an order that is not delivered.

ac-reorder · available

Every item could be added. The cart opens with one notice; what was already in it is kept (O-3).

ac-reorder · item_no_longer_available

Some items cannot be added: each is named with its reason, never silently left out, and the rest are added. Then the case where nothing could be added.
