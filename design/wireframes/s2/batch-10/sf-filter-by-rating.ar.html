sf-filter-by-rating · default

Rating is one more group of the reviewed listing filters, among the others, with nothing chosen: in the mobile and tablet drawer and in the desktop sidebar (IX-01). Drawn on one category listing, Beauty.

sf-filter-by-rating · applied

One level chosen, 4 and up: the list narrows to the products rated at or above 4, its chip joins the active filters, and the list returns to page 1 (DQ-02).

sf-filter-by-rating · no_reviews_yet

No product in the list has a rating yet. Recommended: the group is not shown, as the native block hides itself (A1). Drawn on a newer listing, Wellness › Vitamins and supplements.
