ad-customer-metrics-export Customers, second release: lifetime metrics and data export

Shows staff each customer’s total spend, average order value, returns and coupon usage on the WooCommerce Customers report and the customer’s profile, and lets permitted staff start WordPress’s personal data export for one customer from either place. The standard report, profile and export screen are annotated, not redesigned.
