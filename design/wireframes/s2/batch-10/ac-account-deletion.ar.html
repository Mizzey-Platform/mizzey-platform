ac-account-deletion Account deletion request

ac-account-deletion · default

The entry, asked for “from the account page”: a separate, quiet section at the end of the account details page (ac-account-details), not a navigation row (A7). Then the same entry after an unconfirmed request expired.

ac-account-deletion · request

What a request does, in plain words, before anything is sent: a link is emailed to the account’s address to confirm it (A7); the team handles it once confirmed; the order records the store must keep are kept; the account stays usable until the request is handled. No period is stated.

ac-account-deletion · confirm

An explicit confirmation step. Recommended: a dialog (fb-modal, as batch-06 draws it for deleting an address), a bottom sheet on mobile and centred on desktop (A7).

ac-account-deletion · requested

Just after: “Check your email”, with the date and time the request was recorded. On a later visit, “Deletion requested on 26 October 2026” in place of the entry, awaiting confirmation or confirmed (A7).
