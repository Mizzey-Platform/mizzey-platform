Mizzey wireframes

The low-fidelity phase closed on 2026-10-28. 148 drawn surfaces in 252 files, 148 reviewed, in 10 review batches. Generated from the manifest and the inventory by tools/design_index.py; do not edit this page by hand.

Structure only. Greys, a system typeface and labelled boxes: not the brand, not the interface design, not PRE-03b.
Languages. English is left to right, Arabic right to left; the notes are in English in both.
States. Each file draws every state listed for its surface, at mobile 360 and desktop widths.
Administration. English only, desktop, drawn on the standard WordPress and WooCommerce screens.
