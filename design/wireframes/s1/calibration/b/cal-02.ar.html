cal-02: Mobile header and navigation

Composed like a publication. A centred masthead between heavy rules, display type, numbered sections, a mix of feature and standard product sizes, full-bleed imagery and a typographic category index. It shows the less native, more expressive candidates.

IX-07 Field that expands in the header into a full-width search layer.

gl-header-mobile:search_open — The search icon turns the bar itself into the field; the mark gives way. Suggestions drop under it, over a dimmed page (NAV-09).
IX-07 · Search interaction (mobile)
Shows: Field that expands in the header bar, with a panel under it.
Alternatives: full-screen overlay (A, C).
gl-mobile-nav:open — The menu fills the screen: categories in large type, sign in and wishlist as two halves of a bar, the language choice below (NAV-02, NAV-04, NAV-05).
gl-mobile-nav:nested_level — Clothing opens in place; its children are indented under it, with “Shop all” first.
IX-06 · Mobile menu levels
Shows: Accordion: levels expand in place. Reached from the menu button (NAV-10, fixed).
Alternatives: drill-down (A), two-pane (C).
IX-02 · Mini-cart
Shows: “Cart 2” opens a sheet dropping from the header (cal-08).
Menu.
The accordion keeps the whole tree in one scroll, which suits a shallow catalogue. With three or more levels, A’s drill-down orients better.
The bar mirrors: mark at the right, menu button at the far left. The plus and minus sit at the inline-end (left). Indentation of children is on the right. The keyboard is the system’s.
