Default

The invoice document (ACCT-12): readable on a phone, printable on desktop, with a plain return link around it. Labelled regions for the seller, the invoice’s numbering and date, the customer, the lines and amounts as charged, shipping, tax, the total and the payment method.

Full order

Every region filled: several lines, one of them two units, shipping, the tax line, the total and the payment method.

With discount

A coupon discount: one Discount row with its code, between Subtotal and Shipping, as on the order.

Refunded partly

A partly refunded order: the refund rows and the net paid, after the payment method.

Long content

Many lines: the document breaks onto a second page, repeating what identifies it.
