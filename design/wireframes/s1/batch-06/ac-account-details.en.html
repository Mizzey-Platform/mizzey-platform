One page, Profile and password: the details the account holds (first name, last name, email) and the password change, in one form with one Save, as the platform handles them.

Validation

On Save with a required field empty and an email that is not valid: the error summary, then each field’s message.

Saved

After Save: one success notice; the form shows what is now saved (US-10-05: the change is confirmed).

Email change

US-10-05 is kept (owner, 20 October 2026): a new email takes effect only once it is confirmed from the new address. The platform saves an email change at once (PLATFORM-FACTS §6), so the confirmation is an addition to the platform, built at implementation.
