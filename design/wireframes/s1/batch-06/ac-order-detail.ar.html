Default

One order: status and timeline, tracking, the items and the as-charged summary, the delivery address, the payment method, the invoice. One column on mobile; on desktop the blocks beside the summary.

Each status

The status block for each customer status, as ord-status-timeline and ord-status-labels define them. Pending payment and Payment failed have no timeline: the line starts at Confirmed.

Tracking not yet

Before the order is handed to the carrier: the tracking block says what will appear, and nothing else.

Tracking available

Once shipped: the tracking number, the carrier’s latest status in the contracted words, and a labelled link out to the carrier’s page (VENDOR-BOSTA).

Refunded partly

Part of the order refunded: one refund row per refund (.sm__rf) and the net paid (.sm__t--net) under the payment method.

Refunded fully

The whole order refunded: the refund equals the total, and the net paid is zero.

Cancelled

A cancelled order: the timeline ends at Cancelled (interrupted); no tracking.

Returned to origin

Not delivered and returned: its own label and the end of the line. Never shown as Delivered (ORD-13, SHIP-17, US-09-06).

Invoice available

The invoice action: Download invoice, from the order (ACCT-12, US-10-03).

Invoice not available

Before payment is confirmed: the block says only that the invoice is not available yet.

Long content

A large order: seven lines with long names, a coupon discount, two refunds, a long address.
