Default

Every customer-facing status, by name and shape, greys only: never colour alone.

Pending payment

Order created, awaiting payment (ORD-01).

Payment failed

Payment failed; order not confirmed (ORD-02). Never shown as Paid or Confirmed (US-08-04).

Cancelled

Cancelled with a recorded reason (ORD-08). The reason is staff-facing; it is not shown to the customer.

Refunded

Refunded in full, using the standard WooCommerce refund states (ORD-12).

Partly refunded

Refunded in part (ORD-12). The order’s summary shows each refund and the net paid.

Returned to origin

Delivery failed, returned to origin (ORD-13): a distinct state, never shown as Delivered (SHIP-17, US-09-06).

COD collected, awaiting remittance

A staff and accounting state, drawn as an annotated mapping, not a customer label (DQ-05, locked).
