Default

The wishlist page, /wishlist/ (IA-11): a storefront page under the full header, with the breadcrumbs, the title and the count, then the saved items as the batch-02 product card in the batch-02 grid. Each card keeps its wishlist control and gains two actions: Move to cart and Remove.

Empty

Nothing saved: one route to shopping, as the batch-02 and batch-04 empty states do.

Guest

A temporary list for a visitor who is not signed in (WISH-03), with the route to keep it by signing in or creating an account (CART-17).

Signed in

The kept list of a signed-in customer (WISH-02, ACCT-08). Nothing extra.

Item out of stock

A saved item that is out of stock: the card’s stock line, Move to cart unavailable with the reason stated, Remove kept (US-11-05).

Moved to cart

One action, confirmed in place under IX-02: the header count steps up on success only, one polite announcement, no drawer. The item leaves the list, because the action is a move.

Long content

Long names in both languages, and a long list. The list pages at the listing page size with the batch-02 numbered pagination (DQ-02).
