Default

The entry, for an eligible customer: Write a review in the summary (DQ-06). Signing in alone does not make a customer eligible.

Signed out

The entry only: “Sign in to write a review” opens the batch-04 sign-in page from a review (D4), already drawn.

Form

A star rating (ctl-rating-input) and the review text (ctl-textarea). No title, no name, no email, no upload.

Validation

Both fields missing on submit: the error summary, then each field’s error (fb-inline-validation).

Submitted

The review is sent and hands over to rv-pending: the form is replaced in place by the acknowledgement.

Not eligible

Signed in, without a delivered purchase of this product: one line of plain text in place of the entry says who writes reviews (DQ-06, D-17). No form, button or link.

Already reviewed

Signed in, with a pending or an approved review of this product in either language: one line of plain text in place of the entry says so (DQ-06, D-17). No form, and no edit, delete or second review.
