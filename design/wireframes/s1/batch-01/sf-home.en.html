Home section types

Nine sections, each a complete type from the curated library (Design Brief 4.6). Every one can be hidden without losing its content and without leaving a gap (SSC-03), moved to any position (HOME-11), and is edited separately in English and Arabic (HOME-10). No section depends on its neighbour. Variants are listed only where they are drawn.

Section headings shown in tiles, collections and brands are interface strings, translated, not editor content.

Default

Mobile first. One prominent hero, then trust, categories and the two rails, with the collections, offer, brands and signup between them. Product imagery sits on the page ground; one primary action per section. Section tags are annotations.

Variants

The variants the section library carries, drawn so the editor’s choices are visible.

Quick add on a home rail

IX-02 as refined at the reconfirmation. Shown on trending; new arrivals behaves identically.

A section hidden

Featured collections switched off in the editor. Its content is kept; the page closes up.

An empty rail

A rail whose source has no products renders nothing on the storefront, without a broken region (US-02-01). Each rail has its own empty state.

Loading

Text arrives with the page; images load lazily into reserved boxes. No spinners and no shimmer.

Signup

Marketing consent is captured separately from the signup and is never pre-ticked (US-02-02).

Long content

Long headings, names and labels, in both languages.
