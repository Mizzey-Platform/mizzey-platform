ad-order-cancel Cancel an order with a recorded reason

A panel added to the standard order screen where staff cancel an order with the reason recorded (ADM-90, ORD-08). The stock released to the ERP on cancellation (BR-007) is a sentence, never a control. The two native routes that bypass the reason are annotated, not solved.

ad-order-cancel · default

The standard order screen, muted, with the Cancel order panel at the end of the sidebar. The reason field and the action sit together. The annotation under the screen names the two native routes the panel does not close.

ad-order-cancel · reason_required

Cancel order pressed with no reason: refused. The error notice links to the field, the message is under it, and the order is unchanged.

ad-order-cancel · cancelled

The order is cancelled: the success notice, the status Cancelled, and the record of the reason, who and when in place of the form, with the stock and payment sentences.
