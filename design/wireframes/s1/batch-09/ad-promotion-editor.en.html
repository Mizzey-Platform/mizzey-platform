ad-promotion-editor · default

The standard coupon list with three columns added: whether each is a code or automatic, its start date, and its status as text and a shape.

ad-promotion-editor · create

A new automatic promotion: no code, applied when its conditions are met. The coupon screen keeps its own fields; the added choice, start date and cap sit where they belong.

ad-promotion-editor · scheduled

Saved with a start in the future: the status says Scheduled and when it starts and ends.

ad-promotion-editor · active

Inside its dates and running: Active, how often it has been used, and Pause promotion.

ad-promotion-editor · paused

Paused inside its dates: it stops applying, its usage and the orders that used it are kept, and it can be resumed.

ad-promotion-editor · validation

A start after the expiry date, refused on Update: the error notice at the top links to the field, the message sits under it, and every value is kept.
