ad-refund-record · default

The standard order screen, muted. WooCommerce’s own Refund button under Items, shown to a role that holds the refund capability (ADM-91, as 09-A draws it). Pressing it opens the refund panel with the Payment reference added.

ad-refund-record · full_refund

A full refund through Paymob: every line and the whole amount, Payment reference left empty as the field says for this route. Then the order with the refund recorded, the returned reference shown as a placeholder, nothing left to refund and the status Refunded.

ad-refund-record · partial_refund

A partial refund made in Paymob’s own process and recorded manually: one line, the wallet, with the Payment reference the staff member entered. Then the order with the refund and its reference recorded, EGP 2,000.00 still refundable and the status unchanged.

ad-refund-record · exceeds_paid_amount

A refund above what was paid: refused. The error notice sits in the panel and links to the amount, the message is under it, every value is kept, including the Payment reference, and nothing is refunded.

ad-refund-record · permission_denied

The same order for a staff account without the refund capability: the Refund control is not rendered at all, so neither is the panel or its Payment reference.
