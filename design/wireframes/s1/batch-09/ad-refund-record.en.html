ad-refund-record · full_refund

A full refund: every line and the whole amount available. Then the order with the refund recorded, nothing left to refund and the status Refunded.

ad-refund-record · partial_refund

A partial refund: one line, the wallet. Then the order with the refund recorded, EGP 2,000.00 still refundable and the status unchanged. Where its payment reference is recorded is annotated.

A refund above what was paid: refused. The error notice sits in the panel and links to the amount, the message is under it, every value is kept and nothing is refunded.

The same order for a staff account without the refund capability: the Refund control is not rendered at all, so neither is the panel.
