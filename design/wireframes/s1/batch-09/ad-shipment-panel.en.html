ad-shipment-panel Shipment, tracking and pickup on the order

Annotated, not built: where staff see an order’s shipment today with Bosta’s own plugin (4.5.7) behind the Bosta adapter. ad-shipment-panel · default

Where shipping is seen today, on the screens Bosta’s plugin actually touches: the order screen (its Area dropdown and its side box) and the order list (its columns). Nothing is added.

ad-shipment-panel · not_sent

A Preparing order not yet sent to Bosta: no tracking number, so Bosta’s box renders nothing and the list’s Bosta columns are empty.

ad-shipment-panel · sent_with_tracking

Sent: Bosta returned a tracking number, stored on the order. It is seen in the list’s Bosta Tracking Number column and, where it renders, in Bosta’s box. The order status is unchanged until a mapped carrier status arrives.

ad-shipment-panel · pickup_requested

A pickup requested: it lives on Bosta’s own Pickup Requests page under the Bosta menu, not on the order. The order list still shows the order’s Bosta status.

ad-shipment-panel · carrier_error

A send failed. The adapter reports the failure to the person who sent it (US-09-02); the order keeps its status and gets no tracking number. Bosta’s own sync column says what Bosta reports.
