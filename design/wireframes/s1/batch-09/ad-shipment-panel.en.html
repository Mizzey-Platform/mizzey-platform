ad-shipment-panel Shipment, tracking and pickup on the order

The standard order screen with two additions (owner ruling D1): one Send to Bosta entry in WooCommerce’s Order actions, applied with its Apply button, and a read-only Shipment record showing the tracking number (SHIP-03), the carrier status (SHIP-13) and the pickup (SHIP-12). Bosta’s own pages, columns and box stay as they are.

ad-shipment-panel · default

A Preparing order not yet sent, with the two additions in their resting state: Send to Bosta among the Order actions entries, and the read-only Shipment record at the end of the main column. Bosta’s own elements are muted around them, and the order list keeps Bosta’s columns.

ad-shipment-panel · not_sent

The same order, not yet sent: Send to Bosta chosen in Order actions and Apply about to be pressed. Nothing has happened yet; the record still reads not sent.

ad-shipment-panel · sent_with_tracking

Sent: the success notice, and the Shipment record with the tracking number Bosta returned and its link out. The order is still Preparing until a mapped carrier status arrives.

ad-shipment-panel · pickup_requested

A pickup requested on Bosta’s own Pickup Requests page. The order’s Shipment record shows the pickup state, read only, as Bosta reports it.

ad-shipment-panel · carrier_error

Send to Bosta applied and the send failed: an error notice says so with the reason, the order keeps its status and gets no tracking number, and the Shipment record keeps the failure.
