ad-redirect-manager · default

The screen as it opens: the add form above the list of existing redirects. The form is the shared settings form: label column, field column, description under each field, one action at the end.

ad-redirect-manager · empty

No redirect yet: the form, and the list with the native empty row.

ad-redirect-manager · list

A longer list across two pages, with an Arabic path and a long address, to show isolation and wrapping.

ad-redirect-manager · validation

A loop refused: the redirect is not added, the error notice at the top links to the field, the message sits under the field, and the values entered are kept.

ad-redirect-manager · saved

The redirect is added: a success notice under the title, the form cleared for the next one, and the new redirect first in the list.
