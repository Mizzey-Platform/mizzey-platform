ad-order-status-control · default

The standard order screen, muted. The status select in General is WooCommerce’s own; what is added is the check it passes when Update is pressed.

ad-order-status-control · allowed_change

Confirmed changed to Preparing and Update pressed: the change passes the check. WooCommerce’s own “Order updated.” notice, the new status in the select, the native prose note, and one row in the status history.

ad-order-status-control · refused_change

Delivery failed / Returned to origin changed to Delivered and Update pressed: refused with the reason. The error notice links to the select, the message is under it, the chosen value is kept and the saved status is named.
