ad-two-factor · default

The policy, as the component holds it: two-factor required for the administrator, the shop manager and any staff role; customers excluded; the methods allowed; a grace period, then the account locked.

ad-two-factor · setup

A staff member setting up their own second factor: from their profile, choose a method, link an authenticator app with its code, then keep the backup codes.

ad-two-factor · challenge

After the password, the second step: the code from the app, with the other ways in: a backup code, a passkey, a code by email.

ad-two-factor · wrong_code

A wrong or expired code: the component says so above the field, the field is cleared, and the other ways in stay offered.

ad-two-factor · recovery

Recovery from a lost second factor: the user’s backup code first; otherwise an administrator resets their second factor, or unlocks an account locked at the end of its grace period.
