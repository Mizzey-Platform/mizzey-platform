rp-sales · default

The two standard reports that together hold RPT-01: Revenue (gross, returns, coupons, net, taxes, shipping, total), and Orders (orders, average order value, items). Month to date, as they open.

rp-sales · empty_range

A range with no orders: every Revenue figure reads zero; the chart and the table say there is no data.

rp-sales · date_range

A chosen range, last week, shown by week: the figures, the chart and the table follow it.

rp-sales · exported

Download pressed: WooCommerce prepares a CSV of the table as shown. A small report downloads at once; a large one is emailed.

rp-sales · permission_denied

A role without the reports capability opening the report: CoreX’s standalone refusal, as in the accepted baseline.
