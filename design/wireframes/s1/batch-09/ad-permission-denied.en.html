ad-permission-denied · default

A staff screen the role can use: the standard order screen for a staff account that holds the refund capability. The Refund control is native; the capability check around it is what this surface adds.

ad-permission-denied · control_hidden

The same screen for a staff account without the refund capability. The control is not rendered at all: nothing is greyed out, disabled or explained in place.

ad-permission-denied · action_refused

A screen the role may not open, reached by a bookmark or a typed address: WordPress’s own refusal, HTTP 403. Annotated, not redesigned.
