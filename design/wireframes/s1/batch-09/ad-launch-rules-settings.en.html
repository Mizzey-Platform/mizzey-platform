ad-launch-rules-settings · default

The Launch rules tab as it opens on a configured store: the welcome discount, the free-shipping condition and what may combine, each value a labelled variable.

ad-launch-rules-settings · defaults

Out of the box: the working defaults the build ships with, each labelled as unconfirmed, and nothing combining until it is set.

ad-launch-rules-settings · validation

Two problems refused on Save changes: the welcome discount empty while the rule is on, and a minimum of 0 items. The error notice lists both as links; each message sits under its field; the other values are kept.

ad-launch-rules-settings · saved

Saved: WooCommerce’s own success notice under the title, the values kept. The rules apply from the next cart and checkout.

ad-launch-rules-settings · rule_off

The free-shipping rule switched off: its values are kept but not used, its field disabled, and the cart prompt disappears.
