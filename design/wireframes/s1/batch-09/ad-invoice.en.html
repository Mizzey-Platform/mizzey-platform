ad-invoice · default

The standard order screen, muted, with a Documents panel at the end of the sidebar: print or download this order’s invoice and its packing slip (ad-packing-slip).

ad-invoice · full_order

Print invoice pressed on a full order: the document as it prints, at A4, with labelled regions for the seller, the numbering and the tax lines.

ad-invoice · refunded_partly

The invoice of an order refunded in part: the refunded line marked, then the refund and the net amount paid under the total.

ad-invoice · long_content

A long order: nine lines with long product names and an address entered in Arabic. The lines run onto a second page, which repeats the invoice number and the order.
