ad-promo-banner-manager · default

The header template part with no banner yet. The inserter offers the one section allowed above the header: the promotional banner, as a strip.

ad-promo-banner-manager · create

A banner just inserted: its fields empty, Enabled on, the strip a placeholder on the canvas. It shows nowhere until it is saved with text and a start.

ad-promo-banner-manager · scheduled

Saved with a start in the future: the status says Scheduled and when it starts. The strip stays in the editor; the storefront shows nothing yet.

ad-promo-banner-manager · active

Inside its dates and enabled: the status says Showing, on which page, and until when.

ad-promo-banner-manager · ended

Its end has passed: the status says Ended. Its content is kept, to reuse with new dates, or the banner is removed with the native Delete.

ad-promo-banner-manager · disabled

Switched off inside its dates: Enabled unchecked, the status says Switched off. Nothing shows; the content and dates are kept.

ad-promo-banner-manager · validation

An end before the start, refused: the banner is not saved, the error notice in the editor links to the field, the message sits under it, and every value is kept.
