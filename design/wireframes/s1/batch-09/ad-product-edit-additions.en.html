ad-product-edit-additions · default

The standard product screen with the candidate fields in the places they belong: Compare-at price on General, Product video beside the gallery, the barcode on Inventory (native), and pointers to the search engine panel and the merchandising controls.

ad-product-edit-additions · simple_product

A simple product filled in: a compare-at price above the price, a video beside the gallery, and the barcode in the native GTIN field on Inventory.

ad-product-edit-additions · per_variant

A variable product, Satin lipstick: each shade is a variation with its own SKU, barcode and price. The barcode per variant is the native GTIN field on the variation, a candidate.

ad-product-edit-additions · validation

A compare-at price below the price, refused on Update: the error notice at the top links to the field, the message sits under it, and every value is kept.
