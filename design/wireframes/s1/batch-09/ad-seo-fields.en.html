ad-seo-fields · defaults

Nothing typed: each field shows the value that will be used, marked “Generated”, so staff see what search engines get before changing anything.

ad-seo-fields · overridden

Title and description typed for this product; the canonical left to its own address; indexing left at the default. Saved with the native Update.

ad-seo-fields · arabic_version

The Arabic product, edited on its own: its search engine values right to left, the canonical address left to right, the labels English.

ad-seo-fields · slug_changed

The product’s slug changed in the native Permalink and the product updated: an added notice offers one permanent redirect from the old address, and creating it adds the redirect in Redirection, the single redirect mechanism (ruling R1).
