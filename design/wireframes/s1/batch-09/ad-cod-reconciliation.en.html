ad-cod-reconciliation · default

A cash on delivery order out for delivery: the Cash on delivery panel at the end of the sidebar, nothing collected or remitted yet. No status is shown, as neither state has been recorded.

ad-cod-reconciliation · collected_not_remitted

Delivered and the cash collected by the courier: the order is COD collected, awaiting remittance. The panel shows when it was collected and the amount not yet remitted, as a figure, and the remittance can be recorded.

ad-cod-reconciliation · remitted

The remittance recorded: who recorded it and when, and the amount not yet remitted now nil.

ad-cod-reconciliation · not_applicable

An order not paid by cash on delivery. Drawn with cash on delivery offered and not offered (OD-05): the difference is WooCommerce’s own payment setting; the panel reads the same one line in both.
