ad-section-library · default

The Home page open in the block editor: the nine delivered sections in their reviewed order (sf-home §1 to §9), each one complete, its layout fixed. The page settings are native and muted.

ad-section-library · choose_section

The inserter open on the Home page. It offers the curated sections eligible for this page and nothing else; the insertion point sits between two sections.

ad-section-library · edit_content

The hero selected. The heading is edited in place on the canvas; the settings sidebar holds the fields the hero exposes, and says what the design fixes.

ad-section-library · choose_variant

The hero’s layout changed to full bleed. The choice lists only the variants in the approved design; the canvas shows the new layout.

ad-section-library · hidden_section

The Trending rail hidden with WordPress’s native block Hide, from the block’s options menu. The native Hide block dialog, with Omit from published content. The block leaves the editor canvas and is marked hidden in List View, its content kept; the storefront skips it with no gap.

ad-section-library · reordered

Brand highlights moved up from eighth to fifth. List View and the canvas show the new order; the section itself is unchanged.

ad-section-library · arabic_version

The Arabic Home page, edited on its own in the same editor. Content values read right to left; the editor and its labels stay English.

ad-section-library · preview

A change not yet saved, previewed first. The native Preview menu is open; the preview itself is the reviewed storefront page, not redrawn.

ad-section-library · revert

The last change undone: the native revisions screen compares the version before it, then restores it. The restore is itself a new revision with its author and time.
