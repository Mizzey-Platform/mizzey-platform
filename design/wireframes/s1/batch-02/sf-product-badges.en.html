The four badges, and more than one

Only New, Trending, Limited and Best Seller exist (MER-01, US-14-09), assigned per product by the staff (SSC-22). They sit at the image’s inline-start corner, as text in an outlined pill. The sale saving is a price mark, not a merchandising badge, and keeps its own dark pill beside it.
