DQ-02, provisional: evaluation and recommendation

Numbered pagination is drawn as the working baseline. No alternative screen is drawn. The recommendation is for the owner’s review.

Keep numbered pagination for the first release. It is the only pattern that meets NFR-03 and NFR-11 natively, keeps the footer and its policies one scroll away, restores the place in the list on return without extra work (US-03-04), keeps each page bounded on mobile, and is the platform’s own behaviour (M-4). Its cost, a tap per 24 products on a phone, is met by a generous page size and a clear “Showing 25–48 of 268”. If evidence after launch shows long browsing sessions on mobile, Load More can be added later as an enhancement of these same paged links, as the owner’s adaptation already allows. Not a decision: DQ-02 stays provisional.
