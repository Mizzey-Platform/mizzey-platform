Recommendation: one panel

Chosen on the Arabic layout at 360 px with long names and many values, as IX-01 asks. One solution is drawn; the comparison is reasoning, not a second screen.

Arabic, 360 px frame, 16 px guttersOne panel (drawn)Two panels: a list of filters, then a second level per filter
Width for an option labelAbout 250 px: the frame less the gutters, the checkbox and the countThe same 250 px on the second level, but the group list and its values are never seen together
Long values, such as قمصان وقمصان خارجية بأكمام طويلةOne line, or two at mostOne line on the second level; the first level only shows the group name
Seeing what is appliedEach closed group summarises its choices and count under its heading, all in one viewOnly on the first level; the visitor goes back to check
Steps to change two groupsOpen, choose, open, chooseOpen, choose, back, open, choose, back
Focus and screen readerOne dialog, disclosure buttons, one focus trapTwo levels inside the dialog; focus moves between levels and must be returned on Back
Native baselineMatches the WooCommerce filter overlay, which stacks its blocksA custom second level
One panel, with collapsible groups. At 360 in Arabic it gives every label the full width, shows every applied choice at a glance in the closed groups’ summaries, needs fewer steps, keeps one simple focus trap, and stays close to the native filter overlay. A second level would only help a single filter with hundreds of values, and no category in the staging seed has one. Drawer: from the inline-start edge, the same side as the desktop sidebar, full width on mobile and 400 px on tablet.
