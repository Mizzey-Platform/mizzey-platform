Content states

Image on the page ground, then the name, any rating, the price and, when it applies, the stock line. Badges sit at the image’s inline-start corner; the wishlist control at the inline-end corner. On touch, quick add is always visible; on desktop it appears on hover and keyboard focus (A5).

Stock, image, wishlist and length

Drawn at 360 so the narrowest card is the one judged.

Hover and focus

Desktop shows quick add on hover and on keyboard focus (A5). On touch there is no hover; the controls are always present.

Quick add: adding and added (IX-02, locked)

A successful add is confirmed in place on the card, updates the header count and is announced, without opening the mini-cart and without moving focus away from the quick add. A failed add shows its error on the card, with no success message and no change to the count.

Variable product: the choice is made in place

A product that needs a size or colour opens a small panel anchored to its card. The visitor chooses and adds without leaving the list (US-03-01).
