Adding and added: IX-10, recommended pattern

Recommended: confirm in place. On the product page the visitor is still deciding: a second size, a gift quantity, another look at the photos. A confirmation in the buy area keeps them there, follows the IX-02 rule they already met on listings, keeps focus where it was, and works the same on the mobile bar. The cart and checkout are one press away in the confirmation, and the header cart still opens the mini-cart on a deliberate press. The drawer’s strength, the free-shipping prompt in view, is kept for that deliberate press. Buy Now is unchanged: it goes to checkout with the item (US-04-04).
Inventory note. The state note for sf-pdp-simple:added reads “Mini-cart shown”. It predates IX-10 and does not match this recommendation, which keeps the confirmation inline. For Claude Code to reconcile after the owner’s decision.
