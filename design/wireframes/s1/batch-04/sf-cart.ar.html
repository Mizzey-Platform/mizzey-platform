Default

Mobile first at 390, then the 768 tablet, 1024 and 1280. Lines first, the order summary after them on mobile and beside them from 1024. The suggestions follow.

Updating a quantity

The quantity changes and the totals follow (CART-02). Reserved boxes, no spinner, no layout shift.

Item removed

Remove takes the line out of the cart (CART-03).

Moved to the wishlist

Move to wishlist takes the line out of the cart and saves it (CART-04).

Coupon applied

Coupon entry with a clear success (CART-06, US-05-06, PROMO-12).

Coupon not accepted

A clear reason, the total unchanged (AC-04). The reason tells expired from not eligible from not found (US-05-06).

Free shipping applied

Shipping becomes free per the active rule, with no code change (AC-02, BR-009, SHIP-05).

Empty

The cart page with nothing in it composes the empty cart (sf-cart-empty), drawn in full in its own file.

Signed out

A guest’s cart. Checking out as a guest is never blocked (AUTH-07, FIX-09).

Signed in

A signed-in customer’s cart persists across devices and sessions (CART-09). After a guest signs in, the carts merge (CART-14, CART-18, AC-18).

Long content

Long names and variant labels in both languages, at 360, 390 and 1280.
