Default

Where the prompt sits: at the top of the cart summary, close to the total it affects, and at the top of the mini-cart’s list (CART-07).

Not qualified

The cart is short of the rule: it says how close (AC-01, US-05-04).

Qualified

The cart meets the rule: shipping becomes free, with no code change (AC-02).

Rule off

When the free-shipping rule is disabled in the admin, the prompt is not shown at all.
