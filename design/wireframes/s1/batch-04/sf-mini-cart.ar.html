Open

Opened by a deliberate press of the header cart control, and nothing else.

Closed

After the drawer closes, focus is back on the control that opened it.

Empty

The drawer opened with nothing in the cart.

After an add

What the drawer holds when the visitor opens it after adding an item. The add itself never opens it (IX-02, IX-10).
